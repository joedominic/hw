Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 22, 2026 • Version 1.0

HireEdge (“we”, “us”, “our”) is dedicated to protecting your career information and privacy. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, process, store, and protect your information across our career operating system, resume tailoring services, job discovery pipelines, and interview management platforms.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Credentials: Email address, username, authentication salt/hashes, and session identifiers.
  • Professional & Career Materials: Resumes (PDF, text, DOCX), historical employment records, education, skills, target titles, compensation expectations, work authorization status, and interview preparation notes.
  • Job Pipeline & Activity Data: Saved roles, customized application diffs, submission confirmations, employer responses, and interview scheduling logs.
  • Service Telemetry: Plan quota utilization, API consumption, feature latency metrics, and error diagnostics.
  • Billing Data: Billing contact information and customer identifiers. All credit card details are handled directly by our payment processor, Stripe, Inc.; HireEdge never stores full card numbers or security codes.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process your information strictly to provide, maintain, and optimize HireEdge services:

  • Evaluating candidate-to-job semantic fit and synthesizing bespoke ATS resume diffs.
  • Automating opportunity matching and scraping public job boards according to your configured search profiles.
  • Tracking application pipeline progression and interview milestones.
  • Enforcing subscription tier limits and rate throttling.
  • Delivering authenticated customer assistance (including staff support impersonation protected by immutable audit logging).

3. Artificial Intelligence & Subprocessor Transparency

HireEdge utilizes leading artificial intelligence and cloud infrastructure providers to deliver its services:

Subprocessor Function Data Shared
Stripe, Inc. Payment processing & subscription portal Email, billing address, plan identifiers
OpenAI / Anthropic / Groq Commercial LLM evaluation & tailoring Job listings, candidate experience prompts
Langfuse Operational telemetry & token accounting Metadata-only (tokens, latency, model ID)
Cloud Infrastructure Host Application hosting & encrypted database Encrypted primary database & file storage

Zero Model Training Guarantee: We explicitly prohibit third-party model providers from using customer resumes, prompts, or feedback to train, fine-tune, or improve public AI models.

Telemetry Privacy: Production telemetry is configured for zero-content leakage. Candidate names, phone numbers, email addresses, and resume texts are stripped or redacted before transmission to observability backends.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your career data only as long as your account remains active. You maintain full ownership of your data:

  • Self-Service Erasure: You can permanently delete your account, uploaded resumes, interview notes, and pipeline history at any time from Settings → Account.
  • Automated Purging: Account deletion immediately purges active database records and cascades through user-specific search profiles, embeddings, and media files. Residual encrypted backup snapshots age out within 30 days.

5. Multi-Tenant Security

Every user account operates inside an isolated, owner-scoped multi-tenant architecture. All database queries, pipeline states, and uploaded files require owner-scoped verification. Communications are encrypted in transit via TLS 1.3, and sensitive records utilize encryption at rest.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as GDPR in the EU or CCPA/CPRA in California), you have the right to request access to your data, export your data in machine-readable JSON format, request correction of inaccurate records, or demand complete erasure.
